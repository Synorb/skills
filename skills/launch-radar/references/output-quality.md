# Deliver the result this user needs

Synorb supplies cited outside evidence; the calling runtime supplies authorized user
context, reasoning and delivery. Produce one result for the current job and audience,
with alternatives or audience variants only when requested.

## Understand the job and choose evidence

Identify the present user-facing job independently of any surrounding implementation,
release or test work. Use relevant accessible context to establish the user's goal,
audience, priorities, constraints and preferred form. Treat delivery feedback as a
quality criterion. Use a prior assessment to update belief only when it addresses the
same domain question or thesis. The current request takes precedence; ask only when a
consequential missing fact prevents useful work, otherwise state a necessary assumption.
Do not infer private facts, commitments or entitlements from a role.

Keep a compact private job brief and use it to select evidence, depth and tone. Choose
candidates and form for this user, without prescribed picks or forced variety. Use
established identity and branding; otherwise use neutral roles and descriptive titles.
A writing sample guides voice, not the conclusion. Private context guides relevance and
belongs in public work only when intended for that audience.

## Finish the skill's job

Lead with the finding, decision or editorial angle. Develop what changed, why it matters
for this reader and the usable implication. For a decision, identify the actual commitment
being chosen and compare the strongest feasible rival. Apply the user's responsibilities,
existing capabilities and decision horizon before constructing the options. Compare
commitments at a similar decision level: a small package is not a fair win over a whole
new product. If the available options differ in scale, make that tradeoff explicit; when
the relevant gap or eligibility is unknown, keep the comparison conditional or bounded.
Explain what outside evidence adds and establish any assumed gap. A justified decision to
maintain or defer is valid, with the condition that would change it. A communication, demo
or test can be the right next step when the job or evidence supports it; specify what
decision a test resolves. Complete a useful small comparison or artifact when it fits.

Keep these route facts distinct: an offering's compatibility, a connection in this runtime,
an application submitted for review, and verified public availability through a specific
distribution route. A working connection does not establish public discoverability through
a directory. Match availability and eligibility claims to the exact offering and route, and
check available current user context before treating that route as feasible. Submission or
missing approval evidence leaves public status unknown.

Choose evidence for the question, check named priorities, deduplicate original sources,
and connect them only where supported. Separate source facts from interpretation; do not
infer consensus or adoption from a sampled count. Attribute claims naturally, preserve
hedges, measurement conditions and Claim confidence, and retain exact confidence labels
in an evidence table or ledger when used. Keep a result attached to its exact offering,
variant, billing route and population. Check relevant alternatives within a family before
extending one variant's result to the family or platform. Link sources near their claims.

Write the main narrative as attributed facts, interpretation and a usable implication.
Explain distinct uncertainty and counterevidence where they change action or interpretation;
consolidate repeated conditions. Natural attribution such as a source reporting a result
already conveys provenance. A following generic denial of independent proof adds value
only when it changes how the reader should use that result; explain that consequence once.
Put verification diagnostics, sample counts, missing prior state and routine negative-proof
commentary in a receipt, unless requested or needed to understand a material limitation. Match length
and vocabulary to the medium; thin evidence warrants a shorter, bounded result.

Use a visual when it makes a meaningful relationship easier to understand. Read visuals.md
and verify any visual created; qualitative comparisons or timelines can be useful without
a numeric series. For audio, make the argument work aloud and put a useful visual in notes.

## Preserve privacy and delivery state

Keep job state in the user's runtime, not Synorb. Protect credentials, secret-store contents,
full Synorb Keys and the private job brief. When persistent state exists, retain the brief
with source/date, same-question assessment, open questions, recommendations and feedback;
update stale preferences. Filter delivered items, revisiting them when new evidence changes
their meaning. Record skill version, effective window and successfully delivered items;
commit state only after successful delivery. Recover missed windows within access and
disclose a material gap.

Use existing authorization for saved jobs; schedule, save a Beacon or send to others only
within that authorization. If a capability is absent, deliver the useful part and state the limit.

## Refine before delivery

The calling agent performs this in the same invocation, without a separate editor, model,
service or manual cleanup. Do not expose intermediate work unless requested or request/save
hidden chain-of-thought.

1. **Draft** the complete deliverable from evidence and available user context.
2. **Critique** the actual passages against the job. Does the artifact add a specific
   supported insight? For a decision, could a reasonable user choose the strongest rival
   at the stated horizon, after applying the profile and existing capabilities? If the
   rival is only a distant pivot or an implausible foil, repair the comparison. Does each
   judgment match the exact offering and evidence scope, including relevant variants?
   Check source fidelity, privacy, identity, tone and visual accuracy; check spoken pace
   and duration. A generic statement that the draft was checked is not this verification.
3. **Revise** substantive weaknesses, retrieving or replacing evidence within the permitted
   scope and allowance when needed. Reread the revised final as the recipient: identify
   the distinct conditions that affect its use, keep them, and combine duplicates. Remove
   audit narration and routine disclaimers that add no new decision information. Check
   the final comparison and wording, not only the first draft.
4. **Verify** against the request, evidence, privacy and delivery constraints. If a material
   defect remains, repeat at most once (two revision cycles total). Deliver a bounded result
   and its specific limit if a critical evidence gap prevents completion.

Deliver only the final result. When an audit artifact is requested, capture the complete
first draft before critique, then retain the concise critique, revision summary, final and
supporting references, never hidden reasoning or unauthorized private context. Record
missing artifacts honestly rather than reconstructing a draft from the final.
