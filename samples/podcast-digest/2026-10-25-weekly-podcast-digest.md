### 1. The a16z Show: "The New Startup Playbook | Box CEO Aaron Levie" (2026-10-25)
[Episode](https://a16z.simplecast.com/episodes/the-new-startup-playbook-box-ceo-aaron-levie-I0FN5_nv)

**The argument.** Aaron Levie says AI agents let a 3-10 person team produce the output of a 50-200 person company (stated), and that founders are becoming managers of work done by agents, so the bottleneck moves from doing the work to reviewing it (stated). He says the best way to build an agent startup is to have done the job being automated (stated), and that large incumbents will struggle because their workflows are too rigid (stated). He also says AI does not fundamentally change how tech is distributed (stated). On market size, he says coding agents already created $20B-$40B of market cap in three years (measured).

**Prediction (his, not fact).** Levie expects hundreds of new companies valued at $1B to $10B+ built on automating specific job functions with agents (stated forecast).

**Quote.** "you can be a 3, 5, 10-person team and have the output of a 50 or 100 or 200-person team."

### 2. The a16z Show: "What It Takes to Win as a Founder | Ben Horowitz" (2026-10-24)
[Episode](https://a16z.simplecast.com/episodes/what-it-takes-to-win-as-a-founder-ben-horowitz-AjG_7MSY)

**The argument.** Ben Horowitz calls AI a "probabilistic computer" where earlier computers were deterministic (stated). A paraphrased Claim says AI apps can reach huge user numbers fast because they do not rely on network-effect adoption curves, and that lasting advantage comes from proprietary data and customer possession, not model architecture (both stated, paraphrase). On venture capital, a paraphrased Claim says a VC's main job is giving founders momentum in hiring, fundraising and customers.

**Prediction.** A paraphrased Claim says AI video and entertainment will emerge as a new storytelling medium (stated forecast).

**Quote.** "You don't take out a competitor with an equally good product. You need something dramatically better."

### 3. Sourcery: "Ex-Twitter CEO Says We're Underestimating AI Agents by 10,000x" (2026-10-24)
[Episode](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/sourcery/episodes/Ex-Twitter-CEO-Says-Were-Underestimating-AI-Agents-by-10-000x-e3r0fd5)

**The argument.** Parag Agrawal (named in the Brief headline) argues that agent use of the web is far larger than people assume and that web business models built on human attention will not hold up in an agent-dominated web (stated analysis, paraphrase). Paraphrased data Claims say his company Parallel closed a $100 million Series B last month at a $2 billion valuation, grew from 30 to 120 employees in a year, and that voice agents need web-search latency of 150-200 ms to be acceptable (all stated).

**Predictions (his).** Agents will eventually use the web 5,000x to 10,000x more than humans; for every 10 GPUs spent on model inference, 1 to 2 will go to web search (both stated forecasts).

**Quote.** "for every 10 GPUs the world spends on model inference, we'll spend one to two on web search."

### 4. 20VC: "Why Robotics Will be 100x Bigger than Coding Agents | Inference Will Be a $10TRN Market" with Naveen Rao (2026-10-24)
[Episode](https://thetwentyminutevc.libsyn.com/20vc-why-robotics-will-be-100x-bigger-than-coding-agents-inference-will-be-a-10trn-market-what-no-one-sees-why-software-investors-are-f-today-with-naveen-rao-unconventional)

**The argument.** Naveen Rao (named in the title) says energy supply is the main limit on AI scaling (stated analysis, paraphrase), that pure software investing is becoming less viable than hardware-centric AI investing (stated analysis), and that checking AI output is becoming a bigger bottleneck than producing it (stated analysis, paraphrase). A paraphrased data Claim says one gigawatt of energy infrastructure costs about $50 billion (stated).

**Predictions (his).** The inference market will reach $10 trillion within 10 years; robotics will be 100 times larger than the market for coding agents; the transformer is not the final architecture (all stated forecasts).

**Quote.** "The inference market is going to be a $10 trillion market within 10 years."

### 5. Official All-In Podcast: "AI Cyber Panic, Ken Griffin's Bubble Warning, The Fall of France?" (2026-10-23)
[Episode](https://allinchamathjason.libsyn.com/ai-cyber-panic-ken-griffins-bubble-warning-the-fall-of-france)

**The argument.** On the show, a speaker says 1% of companies account for 90% of AI spending, and that 80% of that 90% comes from unprofitable VC-backed companies (both stated data, direct quotes). Further Claims say enterprises are moving to open-source and open-weight models for explainability and auditability (stated analysis), and that the US economy is currently highly correlated with the AI trade (stated analysis, paraphrase). The Claims do not name who said the spending figures, so I do not attribute them to a person; the episode title names no guest, and the Brief headline names Nikesh Arora as one.

**Quote.** "of that 90%, 80% of them are from unprofitable VC backed companies."

### 6. David Senra: "Competition, Intensity & Building a $115 Billion Global Bank | Nik Storonsky, Revolut" (2026-10-25)
[Episode](https://davidsenra.com/nik-storonsky)

**The argument.** Nik Storonsky (named in the title) says Revolut has automated 90% of its customer support with AI and raised engineer speed by 30% (both measured data, direct quotes). He argues professional bank CEOs are risk-averse and protect their jobs (stated analysis). A paraphrased Claim says Revolut is building a foundation model called "Pragma" trained on transactional data (stated event).

**Prediction (his).** Revolut aims to be the number one bank in at least 100 countries (stated forecast).

**Quote.** "So 90% of all customer support team is automated now."

### Cross-show threads (my reading, not the shows' words)
- **Agents as the new startup layer.** Levie ([a16z](https://a16z.simplecast.com/episodes/the-new-startup-playbook-box-ceo-aaron-levie-I0FN5_nv)) expects many new agent companies, Agrawal ([Sourcery](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/sourcery/episodes/Ex-Twitter-CEO-Says-Were-Underestimating-AI-Agents-by-10-000x-e3r0fd5)) expects agent web traffic to dwarf human traffic, and Rao ([20VC](https://thetwentyminutevc.libsyn.com/20vc-why-robotics-will-be-100x-bigger-than-coding-agents-inference-will-be-a-10trn-market-what-no-one-sees-why-software-investors-are-f-today-with-naveen-rao-unconventional)) expects a $10 trillion inference market. All three are forecasts.
- **Incumbents are slow.** Levie says large companies cannot change workflows fast enough; Storonsky ([Senra](https://davidsenra.com/nik-storonsky)) says professional bank CEOs protect their jobs. Both frame this as the opening for newcomers.
- **A caution on the money.** The All-In episode ([link](https://allinchamathjason.libsyn.com/ai-cyber-panic-ken-griffins-bubble-warning-the-fall-of-france)) says AI spending is concentrated and mostly from unprofitable VC-backed firms. That sits next to the optimistic forecasts above; I read it as a reason to treat them as forecasts.

**Skipped:** the Bloomberg episodes (India foreign policy, a restaurant owner, Bloomberg Money markets and politics) and TBPN (Moderna vaccine trial, phishing test) as off-topic for AI and startups.
